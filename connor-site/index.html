03  —  The story

He started writing during the hardest period of his life. The music is how he got through it.

C.Wood is Connor, a rapper and songwriter who grew up in Huddersfield and now lives in Morley, just outside Leeds.

His songs pair hard-hitting, conscious lyricism with catchy hooks and a flow that's all his own. They talk openly about the stuff most people keep to themselves, because that's exactly why they matter.
