The story

C.Wood is Connor, a rapper and songwriter who grew up in Huddersfield and now lives in Morley, just outside Leeds.

He picked up the pen when things were at their lowest, and he's never written anything he didn't mean. His songs mix hard-hitting, conscious lyrics with catchy hooks and a flow that's all his own, and they talk openly about the stuff people usually keep to themselves.

That honesty is the point. If one track helps one person feel less alone, it's done its job.
